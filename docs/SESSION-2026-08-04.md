## Decisions worth remembering

- **The deep-lane convention is load-bearing, not ceremonial.** Six passes, eight real blockers — every one a promoted claim that looked green but wasn't actually checked. If verification work ever feels "too slow" to deep-review, this session is the counterexample. The cost of a vacuous promoted vector is durable false confidence.
- **Make verification evidence self-validating.** Hand-maintained counts/registries/prose-reasons drift; the conformance harness now derives them and fails-closed. New rule of thumb: if a checker reports a number, that same checker should assert the number stated in the docs.
- **Stage flips need the recorded review evidence, not just the commit message.** The `resource-state` gap was invisible at the commit level (the message said "approve") and only surfaced because the body contradicted it. Review verdicts live in the item body.
- **Cross-model fresh-context review consistently found what same-context missed** — including the sibling call site (pass 2) and the reverse-horizon gap that the implementer's own mutation notes didn't cover.
- **Patchbay-as-hub adapter seams held up under implementation.** The resource-adapter concept admits both pooled `provider_pool` (administration-capable) and `usage_window` (read-only) shapes via open declaration; nothing single-adapter is baked into core. `epic-token-commune-observer` can build on it directly.
