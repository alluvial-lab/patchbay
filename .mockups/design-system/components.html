failure-banner — failure vocabulary mapping

Never collapses to a generic "failed". Each failure term is distinct: denial, unsupported, revoked, timeout, expiration, cancellation, supersession, execution failure.

Distinct failure terms

authorization_denied

Grant does not cover this OperationKind on this target.

unsupported_command

Adapter does not support spawn at delivery.

target_offline

Session endpoint unavailable. Safe to retry.

execution_failed

Target began executing and reported failure.

execution_outcome_unknown

Execution may have occurred. See retry safety.

retry-safety-indicator — failure term × idempotency_strength

Retry safety is derived from the failure term + the adapter's idempotency_strength, NEVER from CommandState alone. Three outcomes: safe / maybe / unsafe.

The matrix
